Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum project size you handle?

We typically engage on projects $2M and above, though we evaluate each opportunity individually. Contact our business development team to discuss your specific needs.

Do you offer design-build contracts?

Yes. Apex offers traditional general contracting (GC), construction management (CM), and full design-build project delivery. We'll recommend the model that best reduces your risk and cost.

In which states are you licensed to operate?

We hold general contractor licenses in Illinois, Indiana, Wisconsin, Michigan, Ohio, Minnesota, Missouri, Iowa, Tennessee, Georgia, Texas, and Florida, with reciprocity arrangements in most neighboring states.

How do I get a preliminary cost estimate?

Submit your project brief using the inquiry form. Our preconstruction team will perform an order-of-magnitude estimate and schedule a discovery call within 48 business hours.
